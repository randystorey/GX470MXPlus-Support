# GX 470 MX Plus Support

GX 470 MX Plus is an iPhone and CarPlay vehicle monitoring application designed primarily for the Lexus GX470.

## Features

GX 470 MX Plus can display vehicle information obtained through a compatible OBDLink MX+ Bluetooth OBD-II adapter, including:

- Engine coolant temperature
- Transmission pan temperature
- Torque converter temperature
- Charging voltage
- Instantaneous fuel consumption

The app also uses the iPhone's motion sensors to provide vehicle pitch and roll information.

## Requirements

- Compatible iPhone
- OBDLink MX+ Bluetooth OBD-II adapter for OBD vehicle data
- Compatible Lexus GX470
- CarPlay-compatible system for CarPlay display

Pitch and roll measurements do not require an OBD-II adapter.
