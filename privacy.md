# GX 470 MX Plus Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 2, 2026**

GX 470 MX Plus is a vehicle monitoring application designed primarily for use with the Lexus GX470.

## Information Collected

GX 470 MX Plus does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app reads vehicle diagnostic information from a compatible OBDLink MX+ Bluetooth OBD-II adapter. This information may include engine temperature, transmission temperature, vehicle speed, engine operating information, charging voltage, and other vehicle diagnostic data.

The app also uses the iPhone's motion sensors to calculate vehicle pitch and roll.

Vehicle and motion information is processed locally on the user's device for the purpose of displaying vehicle information.

## Tracking and Advertising

GX 470 MX Plus does not track users and does not contain advertising.

The app does not use collected information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or tracking.

## Third-Party Services

GX 470 MX Plus communicates with compatible OBDLink hardware for vehicle diagnostic information. The app does not transmit personal information to the developer through this connection.

## Data Retention

GX 470 MX Plus does not maintain a developer-operated database or server containing user or vehicle information.

## Children

GX 470 MX Plus is a vehicle diagnostic and monitoring application and is not directed toward children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the features or data practices of GX 470 MX Plus change.

## Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the developer through the support information provided for GX 470 MX Plus.

GX 470 MX Plus is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lexus, Toyota, OBDLink, or their respective manufacturers.
